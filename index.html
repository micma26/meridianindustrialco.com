Industries

Garland cable runs critical systems in four industries.

Mining and industrial
  • Blasting and shot firing
  • Power to crushers, conveyors and pumps
  • Process control and instrumentation
  • Site-wide fibre and data
Transport
  • Tunnel ventilation and fire detection
  • Rail signalling and communications
  • Traffic control and road monitoring
Communications
  • Backbone and long-haul fibre
  • Data centres and local networks
  • Pre-terminated fibre assemblies
Security and integration
  • Fire alarm and public address circuits
  • CCTV and access control
  • Building automation

Across all four, the brief is the same: cable that keeps working where a failure stops the operation or puts people at risk.
